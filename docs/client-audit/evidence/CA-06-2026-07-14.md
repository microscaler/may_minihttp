## Interpretation

These two fixtures compress to roughly 12–15% of their original payload size, which is a useful
bandwidth signal for bulk responses. The measurements do not establish a production go decision:
they do not include wire bandwidth, process CPU time, representative Sesame-IDAM or BRRTRouter
payloads, peer `Content-Encoding` support, or streaming behavior. Repeat the probe with captured
payloads from each service and compare the transfer savings against CPU and tail-latency cost
before adding response decompression to the client.
